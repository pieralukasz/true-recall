# Introduction

True Recall is an AI-powered flashcard plugin for Obsidian that uses the FSRS v6 spaced repetition algorithm to help you remember what you learn.

## Why True Recall?

Unlike traditional flashcard apps, True Recall is deeply integrated with Obsidian's note-taking workflow. Your flashcards live alongside your notes, making it easy to create, review, and maintain your knowledge base.

### Key Features

- **AI-Powered Flashcard Generation** — Generate flashcards from your notes using AI. Select text and instantly create Q&A cards, cloze deletions, or reversed cards.
- **FSRS v6 Algorithm** — State-of-the-art spaced repetition that adapts to your learning patterns and predicts when you're likely to forget.
- **Image Occlusion** — Create flashcards from images by hiding regions. AI can automatically detect important areas to quiz.
- **Type-in Mode** — Type your answers during review and get AI-powered semantic grading that understands meaning, not just exact matches.
- **Note Types** — Anki-style templates with customizable fields and card templates.
- **Projects** — Organize notes into hierarchical projects with per-project FSRS presets.
- **Natural Language Queries** — Ask questions about your statistics in plain English: "Show me cards I got wrong yesterday."
- **Cloud Sync** — Sync your progress across devices.
- **25+ Dashboard Widgets** — Embed analytics, forecasts, and gamification elements directly in your notes.

## How It Works

1. **Create Flashcards** — Add flashcards to your notes using simple syntax or let AI generate them for you.
2. **Review Daily** — Open the review session and answer cards using spaced repetition.
3. **Track Progress** — View detailed statistics and optimize your learning parameters.

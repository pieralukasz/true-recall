# Quick Start

This guide will help you create your first flashcard and complete a review session in under 5 minutes.

## Step 1: Create Your First Flashcard

Open any note in your vault and add a flashcard using the basic syntax:

```markdown
What is the capital of France?
::Paris
```

The `::` separator divides your card into question and answer.

### Alternative: Use AI Generation

If you've configured AI settings:

1. Select some text in your note
2. A toolbar appears above your selection
3. Click **Basic** to generate a Q&A flashcard

## Step 2: Collect the Flashcard

New flashcard lines need to be "collected" into the database:

1. Open the **Flashcard Panel** (Cmd/Ctrl + P → "Open flashcard panel")
2. Your uncollected flashcard appears in the "Uncollected" section
3. Click **Collect** to add it to your database

You'll see the flashcard move to the main list with a green "New" badge.

## Step 3: Review Your Flashcard

1. Click the **Review** button in the Flashcard Panel
2. Or use the command: "Review flashcards from current note"

The review view opens showing your question.

### Answering the Card

1. Read the question
2. Think of the answer (or press Space to reveal it)
3. Rate how well you remembered:
   - **Again** — Forgot completely
   - **Hard** — Remembered with difficulty
   - **Good** — Remembered correctly
   - **Easy** — Remembered instantly

Based on your rating, FSRS schedules the next review.

## Step 4: Track Your Progress

Open the **Dashboard** (Cmd/Ctrl + P → "Open dashboard") to see:

- Cards due today
- Study time estimates
- Your notes with flashcards
- Review heatmap

## Tips for Beginners

1. **Start small** — Create 5-10 cards per day at most
2. **Review daily** — Consistency is key for spaced repetition
3. **Be honest with ratings** — FSRS learns from your patterns
4. **Use AI generation** — Save time on card creation
5. **Check the dashboard** — Stay motivated with progress tracking
